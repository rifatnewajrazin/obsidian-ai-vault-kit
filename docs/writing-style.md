# Writing Style

How written content should sound when an AI writes or rewrites it. This covers prose and copy. Note structure lives in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

## Voice

- Concise, frank, conversational. Not corporate. This matters most for cover letters, applications, captions and anything written in the user's own voice.
- Plain words over inflated ones. Short sentences over long ones.
- Keep the user's meaning. Do not turn a strong opinion into a balanced one.

## Habits to ban

These give AI writing away. Cut them.

- "It is worth noting", "it is important to remember", "in today's fast-paced world".
- Lists of exactly three adjectives or three examples used as padding.
- Strings of hedges: "may potentially perhaps".
- A closing sentence that only repeats the paragraph.
- Opening by praising the question.
- Words like "delve", "tapestry", "landscape", "leverage", "seamless", "robust" when a plain word works.
- Headlines in the form "Not just X, but Y".

## Punctuation

- No em dashes and no en dashes in newly written text. Use a comma, a period, a colon, parentheses, or a plain hyphen with spaces around it.
- No double hyphen standing in for a dash.
- No emojis inside prose or copy. Emojis are fine for labelling folders in your note app.

## Language

Input can be in any language or a mix. Output is clean, natural writing in the target language. Rewrite, proofread, fix spelling.

## Leave source material alone

These rules do not apply to source code, command flags, URLs, exact quotations, verbatim transcripts or imported records. Keep that text as it is and label it. Put any cleaned-up summary next to it, not over it.
